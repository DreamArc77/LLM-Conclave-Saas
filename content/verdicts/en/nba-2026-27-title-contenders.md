Their primary competition is expected to come from the Boston Celtics, Denver Nuggets, and San Antonio Spurs, with the New York Knicks and Minnesota Timberwolves trailing as conditional threats. ## Where the council agreed
* **Oklahoma City as the definitive favorite:** The Thunder possess the ideal age curve for 2026–27, with Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Jalen Williams, and Chet Holmgren hitting their physical primes together.
* **Boston's enduring status:** The Celtics remain a consensus top-tier contender, provided their core (specifically Jayson Tatum) avoids major injury attrition.
* **Asset-backed roster insulation:** OKC is uniquely positioned to manage second-apron collective bargaining agreement (CBA) constraints, using their massive draft-pick stockpile to refresh cost-controlled depth or execute targeted upgrades.

## Risks and caveats
* **High-variance projection window:** Forecasting two offseasons in advance carries significant uncertainty, as unforeseen superstar trade demands or contract disputes can rapidly remake the competitive hierarchy.
* **Injury fragility and health cycles:** Key contenders like Boston, Denver, and San Antonio rely heavily on individual superstar health, where a single major injury completely alters championship viability.
