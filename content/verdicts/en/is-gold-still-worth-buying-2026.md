## Council verdict

Four frontier models debated whether gold remains a sensible allocation in 2026. The sample structure below mirrors a real report:

- **Key findings** — each participating model states its position with reasoning
- **Cross-examination** — models challenge each other's assumptions across rounds
- **Consensus** — the council converges on a balanced recommendation
- **Risks and caveats** — dissenting views are preserved, not averaged away

## Why this format

A single model answers in one pass. A council answers in rounds: models read each other's arguments, correct errors, and refine their positions. The report captures the convergence and the remaining disagreements.
